# Version 1.7.0 — Story category and rating in EPUBs and audiobooks

## Changes

Each downloaded story's category and rating are now recorded in its EPUB: for example Literotica's **Loving Wives** category and its average reader rating. FanFicFare's values are written into the EPUB's package metadata whether or not the INI includes a title page, so they survive personal INIs that turn the title page off. Updating a book refreshes them. FanFicFare EPUBs downloaded before this release fall back to the labelled lines on their title page, so books already in a library work without downloading again.

Every audiobook carries them:

- **MP3s** (single and per-chapter) get standard ID3 frames that Music, QuickTime, and other players show: a comment such as "Category: Loving Wives · Average rating: 4.43", the category as grouping, and labelled Category, Rating, and Average Rating fields.
- **M4B files** get the same comment and grouping.

The category and rating also appear under each download, in the open book's summary, and in `epub-audio inspect`. Series EPUBs keep each volume's category once and the first volume's ratings.

The About panel now shows the Labs byline and a link to [StrixMCS Labs on GitHub](https://github.com/strixmcs-labs).

## Fixes

FFmpeg's MP3 encoder stored comments as user-defined `TXXX` frames that players don't show, so the AI-narration note in earlier MP3s was effectively hidden. The app now writes a standard comment frame after encoding, leaving the audio unchanged.

## Requirements

Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. Python, FanFicFare, and a standalone FFmpeg/LAME encoder are bundled. Provider keys stay in macOS Keychain.
