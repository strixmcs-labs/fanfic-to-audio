# Version 1.6.0 — StrixMCS Labs design, an open queue during conversion, and FanFicFare 4.63.0

## Changes

Conversion no longer halts the rest of the app. **Download by Link** stays available while a single export or the queue runs, and downloads no longer lock the audiobook side. Every finished EPUB joins the queue; while the queue runs, each one joins as soon as it finishes downloading, and the queue keeps converting until no waiting books remain. Waiting books can be added, moved, retried, or removed during a run; only the book being converted is locked. A notice confirms additions instead of switching tabs.

The interface adopts the StrixMCS Labs design system for native Labs apps: system appearance and typeface, a white field with Labs oxblood in light mode and the black field with Labs wine in dark mode, 3–6px radii, hairline depth without shadows, oxblood hover edges, a gold keyboard focus ring on app buttons, line icons, neutral and accent status badges with a dot instead of status colors, normal case, and interface text in third person or imperative.

FanFicFare is updated to 4.63.0.

## Requirements

Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. Python, FanFicFare, and a standalone FFmpeg/LAME encoder are bundled. Provider keys stay in macOS Keychain.
