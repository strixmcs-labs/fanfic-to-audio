# Version 1.6.1 — Create Audiobook resizing and the Now narrating panel

## Fixes

The Create Audiobook tab now resizes with the window. In 1.6.0 its content stayed in a narrow centered column under a full-width header, and the queue list stayed 240 points tall. The Open Book and Queue panes now widen with the window, left-aligned under the header, up to a comfortable maximum; the queue list grows with the window; and the text preview is taller. Voice & Text keeps a form width.

## Changes

While the queue runs, a **Now narrating** panel shows the book being converted: its cover with spine and page-edge shading, chapter ticks that fill as each section is narrated, the current section and passage, progress, listening time, word count, and narration settings. The Reading order sidebar marks the section being narrated. The selected book's actions sit in one toolbar, with rarer ones under **More**, and the queue shows its counts beside its title. Short status text drops trailing periods.
